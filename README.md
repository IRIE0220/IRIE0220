## What I build

- **Architecture.** Monoliths and microservices, CQRS, and event-driven systems,
  chosen to fit the product rather than the trend.
- **Real-time video and calling.** WebRTC apps with STUN/TURN (ICE) servers and media
  servers such as Jitsi and Kurento; OTT streaming.
- **Telephony and AI voice agents.** Twilio (including SIP trunking) and RingCentral
  APIs; voice agents built with ElevenLabs and Vapi.
- **Fintech and payments.** Stripe and other payment gateways, digital-asset custody
  with SFOX, and vault management with Utila.
- **Blockchain.** Smart contracts in Solidity on Ethereum and other chains, and Solana
  programs.
- **Web and mobile apps.** Angular, React, Ionic and React Native.
- **Games and 3D.** Unreal Engine, including WebRTC streaming, and Unity.
- **DevOps.** AWS, Azure and DigitalOcean, with my own CI/CD built on Docker images and
  containers across GitHub, GitLab, Bitbucket and AWS pipelines.
